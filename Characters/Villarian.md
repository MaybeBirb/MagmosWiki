Villarian, or Velsarren, is a Magmosian Veldaron working within the MDF, currently in a relationship with Hornet.

### History
Villarian was very nearly an only child growing up, having only his sister Tarresha as a sibling. While Tarresha joined the Drelanxes, Villarian progressed along the standard path for Magmosian soldiers, though was eventually promoted to Infiltrator status alongside [Celian](Celian.md) when the two of them singlehandedly destroyed a PCA MT without any mechanized support. At some point, Villarian was selected to be a Veldaron for his exceptional loyalty, and worked with [Ferris](Ferris.md) as his Vespadon, alongside another Veldaron, [Vakzajevent](Vakzajevent.md).

Villarian was one of the Infiltrators assigned to destroy PCA Captain Breaker's Heavy Warship. He attacked the warship's bridge, killing everyone inside before moving to the main hangar to confront the recently-returned PCA officers. However, when he arrived, he was met by the revelation that the opfficers had reentered their machines, giving him no chance of success. He therefore surrendered.

In prison, he was visited by many PCA officers, chiefly Hornet and Thunder. Villarian attempted to fin out everything he could about humanity and how to fight them, though when he saw images of earth, he was in such disbelief that such a place existed that he jokingly offered to teach Hornet the Magmosian language if she brought him there.

Some days later, Villarian was brought to Ulaanbaatar, Earth under strict supervision from PCA SP, Hornet, Thunder, and several other high-ranking officers, where he stayed the night. Stunned by experiencing an environment that was not hostile, volcanic, and dead, Villarian reluctantly taught Hornet much Magmosian after returning to the prison.

### Traits and Equipment
Villarian is somewhat of a [tsundere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsundere), presenting himself as callous and aggressive. He is, however, still reasonable. Over time, he has grown a strong affection for Hornet (who is now his girlfriend), being deeply protective of her.

He has standard Infiltrator augmentations, and wields a standard Infiltrator sword. Recently, he has also acquired a Brekk, a squared cape that covers both shoulders.

### Behind the Scenes
Villarian was never intended to be a legitimate character, but rather, was supposed to be a throwaway NPC that would die very quickly. At the time of his introduction, he was simply called "Fourth" in the GM's mind.

Originally, his relationship with Hornet started as a joke, which evolved into collective hazing in canon. Ironically, this hazing is what led to their relationship.
