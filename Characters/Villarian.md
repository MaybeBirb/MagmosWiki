# Villarian, Magmosian Infiltrator
---  
*"I 𝘬𝘯𝘰𝘸 this is what I want, but… my entire life, this was portrayed as something worth 𝘭𝘢𝘶𝘨𝘩𝘪𝘯𝘨 at. It was a joke to think about liking a human- an insult. And not a particularly lighthearted one."*
- Villarian

Villarian, or Velsarren, is a Magmosian Veldaron working within the MDF, currently in a relationship with Hornet.

### History
Villarian was very nearly an only child growing up, having only his sister [Tarresha](Tarresha.md) as a sibling. While Tarresha joined the Drelanxes, Villarian progressed along the standard path for Magmosian soldiers, though was eventually promoted to Infiltrator status alongside [Celian](Celian.md) when the two of them singlehandedly destroyed a PCA MT without any mechanized support. At some point, Villarian was selected to be a Veldaron for his exceptional loyalty, and worked with [Ferris](Ferris.md) as his Vespadon, alongside another Veldaron, [Vakzajevent](Vakzajevent.md).

Villarian was one of the Infiltrators assigned to destroy PCA Captain Breaker's Heavy Warship. He attacked the warship's bridge, killing everyone inside before moving to the main hangar to confront the recently-returned PCA officers. However, when he arrived, he was met by the revelation that the opfficers had reentered their machines, giving him no chance of success. He therefore surrendered.

In prison, he was visited by many PCA officers, chiefly Hornet and Thunder. Villarian attempted to fin out everything he could about humanity and how to fight them, though when he saw images of earth, he was in such disbelief that such a place existed that he jokingly offered to teach Hornet the Magmosian language if she brought him there.

Some days later, Villarian was brought to Ulaanbaatar, Earth under strict supervision from PCA SP, Hornet, Thunder, and several other high-ranking officers, where he stayed the night. Stunned by experiencing an environment that was not hostile, volcanic, and dead, Villarian reluctantly taught Hornet much Magmosian after returning to the prison.

While Thunder's visits became gradually more about gleaning information about Magmosian culture, Villarian developed mixed feelings about Hornet, with her visits becoming more as a friend would visit. When Thunder was killed during Operation Iota IV, Villarian was left without any present friends, growing fairly close to Hornet. Other Magmosian inmates caught wind of this, however, and ridiculed him for it, being interested in a human being considered laughable and shameful in Magmosian society. This, combined with Hornet receiving similar rebuke from her human comrades, led to it taking several months more for either of them to confess to even mixed emotions. Over the rest of his stay, this developed into a form of actual romantic affection.

Nearly one year after being captured, the MDF stages a rescue for Villarian and the other inmates, breaking them out of the prison. On his way back, Villarian spoke with Tarresha, who similarly criticized him for his liking of Hornet. Upon arrival at the MDF base, he was given a Brekk, signifying a position of temporary importance, and was brought to testify about all he had learned before Hirakk Vakrass and some Magmosian leadership.

Due to previous communication, Villarian was permitted to visit Hornet in a no-mans-land, where he briefly brought her to the MDF city under strict supervision to see the sights, also granting them one of their first moments of privacy for a short time (which was violently interrupted by Tarresha). The two continued to meet in no-man's-land for a short time after that, providing emotional support to one another throughout the war despite constant MDF surveilance.

At some point, Villarian and Hornet were permitted by the MDF to stay in a temporary housing for a few hours under the pretense of privacy, though cameras were hidden throughout the room. When Hornet discovered one of these cameras, Villarian was deeply reluctant to remove or damage it, despite the MDF having lied about it. Eventually, however, he destroyed it himself, a moment of privacy being more important to him than total loyalty. For this, when he returned, he was called to Hirakk Vakrass's office and nearly killed in punishment.

Hornet took quick notice of this in their next visit, and in the one after that, then despite Villarian being highly reluctant and scared, he eventually agreed to go back with her back to the prison. There, he quickly realized how much he hated confinement.

### Traits and Equipment
Villarian is somewhat of a [tsundere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsundere), presenting himself as callous and aggressive. He is, however, still reasonable. Over time, he has grown a strong affection for Hornet (who is now his girlfriend), being deeply protective of her.

He has standard Infiltrator augmentations, and wields a standard Infiltrator sword. At some point, he also acquired a Brekk, a squared cape that covers both shoulders.

### Behind the Scenes
Villarian was never intended to be a legitimate character, but rather, was supposed to be a throwaway NPC that would die very quickly. At the time of his introduction, he was called "Fourth" in the GM's reference documents.

Originally, his relationship with Hornet started as a joke, which evolved into collective hazing in canon. Ironically, this hazing is what led to their relationship.
