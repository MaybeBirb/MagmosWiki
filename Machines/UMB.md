40m autonomous heavyweight machine. Observed to be deployed for destruction of locations or large targets more than against standard craft.

Equipped solely with three-fingered claws at the end of each arm, instances of Unmanned Machine B rely on fisticuffs as their exclusive form of attack. Recent instances have been observed to use thrusters on their backs.

Unmanned Machine Bs are heavily armored, but struggle against lighter opponents. Damage to their three optical sensors has proven effective in the past.
