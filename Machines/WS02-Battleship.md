A large vessel designed for ship-to-ship combat. Though superior to PCA Battleships in combat, then like all MDF ships, it is incapable of space operation.

Battleships are lined with armaments, including three dorsal grenade cannons capable of tearing warships apart. The front of the ship is equipped with an energy cannon.

Battleships are vulnerable to damage to their rear thrusters and their frontal energy cannon. Destroying the thrusters first will force the ship to land, converting it into a stationary fortress until the frontal cannon is destroyed.
