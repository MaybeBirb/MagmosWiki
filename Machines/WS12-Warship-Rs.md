Redsteel-reinforced warships of the MDF. Exceedingly rare, only three MDF fleets are known to utilize these, usually as troop transports.

These warships have a defensive role, carrying few more weapons than a PCA AS07 Heavy Warship. Regardless, they are nigh-indestructible, even with heavy artillery fire. Conventional weaponry is unlikely to damage them, as even their thrusters are defended by a Redsteel grate.

Given their rarity, it is unlikely any personnel are to fight them. In the event they are present, leave their destruction to the heavier PCA aircraft.
