Relatively small, 4-armed hybrid ground-air machines. Intelligence suggests their designs are reverse-engineered from the Falorians.

Though lacking in durability, these machines attempt to swarm targets in medium numbers and overwhelm them with large quantities of weapons fire, both hovering in the air and on the ground.

Best practices for destroying RAPTOR machines usually involve wide-swinging melee weapons or grenades.
