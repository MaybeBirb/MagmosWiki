25m autonomous reverse-joint, using reverse-engineered technology from the PCA. The lightest class of Unmanned Machine observed thus far.

Instances of Unmanned Machine C appear to utilize incendiary internal machine guns, alongside being capable of deploying a BALTEUS-like hail of missiles. Many have Redsteel plating along their bodies, effectively nullifying the shortcomings of their being reverse-jointed, and they use claws on their arms for more concentrated attacks.

However, despite their missiles, instances of Unmanned Machine C are duelist machines, and struggle with crowd control.
