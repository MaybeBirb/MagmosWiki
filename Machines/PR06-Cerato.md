Thin but large-cored digitigrade machine used by the MDF as suicide bombers. Typically autonomous.

CERATO units are equipped with anti-armor weaponry, including pile launchers and needle missiles, to destroy enemy shields and prevent them from hiding when the CERATO units self-detonate. The explosion is quite sizable, and results in a large pool of lava being created, damaging even long-standing structures.

Destroying CERATO units is fairly easy, though forcing them to cease self-combusion requires a vast quantity of firepower once they have started, typically achievable through overloading their ACS.

### Behind the Scenes
The picture used for the CERATO was previously used for a boss in Destroy the ASDF (or Suicide Mission), as an unknown member of the War Council to try and stop Archon Squad.
