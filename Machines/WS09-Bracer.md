Piloted fighter jets often deployed alongside larger ships for cover, or independently for strafing runs. In either case, they will usualy circle the battlefield for periods of time before moving to attack at opportune moments.

Bracers utilize two front-mounted machine guns, which they use for strafcing and dogfights, though are also equipped with fast-loving split missiles for longer ranges. Some have been seen to use flares.

Destroying Bracers is usually a matter of waiting for them to approach, then overwhelming them with rapid-fire weapons.
